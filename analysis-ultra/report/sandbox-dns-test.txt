5. Why BD still does not flow here
----------------------------------
BD's fork takes `-pubkey prince` (a label, not the 32-byte X25519 key).
Candidates tried through 8.8.8.8 on sd-de1: sha256("prince"), sha256("BD NET VPN"),
and the two 64-hex constants found in libxdnstt.so -> KCP session opens, Noise
handshake never completes. Their own libxdnstt.so (arm64, run under qemu in CI)
is what carries traffic; the sandbox has no qemu and no arm64 sysroot.

6. Interpretation
-----------------
A tunnel domain that answers only inside the carrier network (transparent DNS
hijack: the carrier resolves it, the rest of the internet returns NXDOMAIN /
SERVFAIL) is exactly what Ultra's catalogue describes: 49 of its 56 resolvers are
carrier/eSIM resolvers. From any cloud runner, public resolver or desktop ISP the
zone simply does not exist. BD's zone, by contrast, answers from 8.8.8.8.
